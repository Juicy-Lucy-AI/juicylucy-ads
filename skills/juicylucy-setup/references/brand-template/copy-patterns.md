# <Brand> — copy patterns

Approved, performance-proven copy patterns for this brand. The *method* of
writing compliant ad copy is the engine's (`static-copywriting`); this file is
what that method has already produced and approved for <Brand>. Patterns here
are structural models, not mandatory copy — and none of them overrides
[`product-truth.md`](./product-truth.md) or
[`compliance-overlay.md`](./compliance-overlay.md).

A new brand starts with this file nearly empty. That is correct: a pattern is
approved by performance, not by writing it down first. Fill the sections in as
batches ship and the account reports back.

## Protected terms

These stay in Latin letters in every localization, regardless of target
script, unless a market's established brand treatment says otherwise:

`<Brand>` · `<product name>` · `<any third-party name the copy cites>`
